Skyfleet
Type: Covert Research and Tactical Organization
Location: [[Beruda]]
Primary Authority: [[Jaxon Irongear]]
Base of Operations: [[Sky Docks]], north of the [[Grand Workshop]]
Secrecy Status: Classified (limited personnel have full clearance)

⸻

Overview

Skyfleet is a covert aerial initiative headquartered in [[Beruda]], organized under the leadership of [[Jaxon Irongear]]. It functions as both a research and development enterprise and a tactical response unit, focused on the creation and deployment of aerial vessels powered by arcane and elemental technologies.

Though unaffiliated with any single noble house or military power, Skyfleet operates with limited but high-level support from trusted allies across [[Iedas]]. It remains off-record in all official city documents and is protected by a web of magical wards, false documentation, and misinformation campaigns.

⸻

Purpose

Skyfleet was established to address three major concerns:
	•	The awakening of powerful [[elemental lords]] and their destabilizing effects on regional mana fields.
	•	The fluctuating behavior of leyline currents across Iedas.
	•	The inability of traditional political and military structures to mount coordinated responses.

Its broader goals include:
	•	Development of sustainable airborne transportation and surveillance platforms.
	•	Deployment of specialized teams for magical artifact retrieval, reconnaissance, and field stabilization.
	•	Creation of a flexible, non-national defense framework to respond to magical threats.

Skyfleet operates under an internal doctrine of progress-oriented neutrality. It is structured to act when others cannot—not to impose authority, but to fill operational gaps left by existing powers.

⸻

Organizational Structure

Skyfleet is divided into three primary divisions:

Engineering Corps
Location: [[Sky Docks]]
Function: Designs, constructs, and maintains aerial vessels, propulsion systems, and communication tools. Includes teams of magical engineers, artificers, and logistical experts.
Notable Assets:
	•	The Skyfleet Rings (magical sending devices used by units like [[Team Axel]]).
	•	Mana-buoyant crystal matrices for lift.
	•	Anti-scrying hull designs and layered illusion barriers.

Reconnaissance and Operations
Function: Field deployment teams sent across Iedas. Missions include artifact recovery, elemental disruption, covert observation, and rescue.
Prototype Team: [[Team Axel]]—formed from adventurers not native to Iedas.
Additional Teams: In development. Recruitment protocols are internal and unpublicized.

Arcane Oversight
Function: Monitors arcane stability aboard vessels, reviews operational ethics, and inspects projects for threats to the [[Weave]].
Oversight Framework: Influenced by past scholars such as [[Eldorath Skywatcher]] and [[Hieron Vass]] (deceased). Includes protocols for mana saturation thresholds, spell engine containment, and leyline interference resolution.

Key Personnel and Allies

[[Jaxon Irongear]]
Founder and director. Coordinates all major strategic operations and serves as final authority on fleet doctrine, project approval, and personnel deployment.

[[Otto]]
Initial liaison to Team Axel. Delivered the lumber contract in [[Easthaven]]. Returned to Skyfleet after the mission’s completion. Handles scouting, external messaging, and onboarding logistics.

Otto’s Twin
A public member of the Grand Workshop. Participates in general workshop tasks, occasionally assisting with low-level Skyfleet logistics. No special status or clearance.

[[Gebura]]
Former Silver Sword, now Skyfleet Fixer. Responsible for covert interventions, containment operations, and redirection of unwanted attention. Loyal to Jaxon Irongear.

[[Thalos Drayton]]
Independent arcane researcher housed in the [[Floating Cloud Castle]], which is supplied and coordinated through Skyfleet channels. Specializes in elemental and altitude-based magicule behavior.

[[Nell]]
Silver dragon and weather mage. Provides airspace shielding, meteorological support, and the Floating Cloud Castle itself for Skyfleet research missions.

[[Twin Wizards]]
Two arcane theoreticians who serve as consultants and peer reviewers within the Arcane Oversight division.
	•	The first adheres to classical wizarding traditions—long beard, staff, and robes.
	•	The second favors modern academia—trimmed appearance, spectacles, structured robes.
Their function is to represent and challenge competing magical worldviews through formal debate and experimentation.

Secrecy and Containment Protocols
	•	Location Concealment: The [[Sky Docks]] are magically and mundanely disguised as a hazard zone.
	•	Personnel Cloaking: Operatives use codenames; identities are compartmentalized based on mission clearance.
	•	Information Control: Unauthorized knowledge results in memory modification, magical geas, or—in extreme situations—permanent relocation.

⸻

Historical Notes
	•	Skyfleet began informal development approximately 5 years ago, with early prototypes tested in isolated mountain ranges west of Beruda.
	•	Funding and political coverage were secured after Jaxon Irongear presented an airlift response plan to selected nobles.
	•	[[Team Axel]] became the first successful operational field unit following the [[Easthaven]] lumber contract, which was a disguised supply run.
