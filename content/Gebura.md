Gebura is the reconstructed fragment of a once-whole soul — the half that remained after the Silver Sword known as Kali underwent a metaphysical division. While Kali retained her original memories, blessing, and identity, Gebura was born from the parts left behind: obedience, loyalty, and the capacity for relentless execution.

Though technically a new consciousness, Gebura was not born as an infant but as a fully formed adult, carrying muscle memory and behavioral frameworks from a life she never lived. She is considered a construct of continuity, created not by artifice or ritual, but by a willful act of magical division facilitated by [[Jaxon Irongear]].

Origins

Originally, there was only Kali — a Blessed Child born on the [[Plane of Radiance]] and chosen to serve as a Silver Sword of Gwyn. As she matured, Kali’s growing independence and internal conflict led her to question the purity of duty and the rigid moral compass imposed by the order. Seeking to retain her power but reject the identity forced upon her, she approached Jaxon Irongear to enact a split of self.

In the aftermath, Kali departed, unburdened and unknown, while Gebura remained, formed from the traits Kali left behind — not in shame, but in liberation. Gebura was entrusted to Irongear, who recognized her potential as a tool of absolute precision and loyalty.

Nature and Personality

Gebura is unflinchingly efficient, quiet, and composed. Her demeanor is shaped by purpose — not curiosity or freedom. She rarely speaks unless necessary, and when she does, it is direct and without embellishment. Though she is technically a new person, she carries residual patterns from her origin: posture, reflexes, and ways of thinking that mirror the Silver Sword she once was.

Despite her reserved exterior, Gebura is not cold. She does not lack emotion; she merely suppresses it beneath layers of protocol and instinct. She does not dream of who she was or who she could be. She exists in the space between — the person made from wha
t was discarded.

The Imprint of the Hundredfold

Though she did not inherit Kali’s [[Blessed children|Blessing of the Hundredfold Experience]], a residue remains. This remnant is not conscious, nor does it grant her true simulations — but her body recalls actions that once led to success, as though shaped by thousands of unseen repetitions.

This phenomenon is called the Imprint of the Hundredfold, and it manifests as:
	•	A sudden adjustment of stance mid-strike,
	•	An instinctive gesture before casting,
	•	A pause before a word that makes all the difference.

Gebura describes it as “knowing what I would have done, if I had ever done it.”

These moments of precision are not powers she invokes, but reflexes she cannot explain. It is as if the body she wears remembers victories it never earned.

Role in Skyfleet

Under the command of [[Jaxon Irongear]], Gebura serves as a Fixer — a discreet operative, problem-solver, and enforcer within [[Skyfleet]]. She is often dispatched to untangle diplomatic threats, secure stolen inventions, or eliminate obstacles before they become known to others.

Her presence is not recorded in public rosters. Outside of Jaxon’s inner circle, few know she exists. Fewer still would dare question her appearance when she does arrive — cloaked in anonymity, moving like a shadow dressed in purpose.

Relationship to Kali

Officially, there is no connection. The Silver Sword once named Gebura is gone — her name struck from the archives, her sword now powerless and left behind. The Radiant Order makes no mention of her. Those who trained alongside her would find no trace of her in any official record.

And yet, those who meet her often feel the edge of a forgotten memory, a sense of familiarity — like seeing the reflection of a person long gone in a mirror slightly warped.

Whether Gebura considers herself a person, a fragment, or something else entirely is unknown. If she has thoughts on Kali — the other half, the one who left — she has never spoken them aloud.
