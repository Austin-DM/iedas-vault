# Nell

*Also known as: *Mistress Nell, The Quiet Tempest  
*Species*: Dragon (in disguise)  
*Occupation*: Weather mage, arcane engineer, Skyfleet consultant  
*Primary Location*: [[Beruda]] and the [[Floating Cloud Castle]]  
*Affiliation*: [[Skyfleet]], closely allied with [[Jaxon Irongear]]

---

## Overview

Nell is a powerful silver dragon who has chosen a life of quiet scholarship and arcane engineering over hoarding or rulership. Her chosen name is modest—Nell—a simple word that deflects suspicion, hiding the immense age and power she carries behind a calm, unassuming demeanor. Most know her as a weather mage employed by the Skyfleet, but few realize she is the architect of the atmospheric systems stabilizing their airborne platforms.

She is patient, private, and exacting in her work, preferring subtle influence to open authority. When she does act, however, it is with terrifying precision and elemental force.

---

## Personality

Nell is:

- Dryly witty, fond of metaphor and understatement
- Intensely observant, rarely surprised
- Distrustful of pomp or reckless displays of power
- Loyal to those who value intellect, restraint, and vision

Though she dislikes confrontation, she is not above leveling a mountain to stop a threat. Her power manifests in calculated, large-scale effects—shifting pressure systems, redirecting weather, grounding fleets—rather than duels or explosions.

---

## History

- Originated from the far eastern mountains of [[Iedas]]
- Grew fascinated with atmospheric magic and mana flow
- Met [[Jaxon Irongear]] while saving him from a failed storm conduit experiment
- Helped design and construct the [[Floating Cloud Castle]]
- Now works behind the scenes to maintain [[Skyfleet]] operations around [[Beruda]]

---

## Role in the Present

Nell functions as:

- Chief engineer of Skyfleet’s *atmospheric stabilization systems*
- Arcane meteorologist monitoring high-altitude mana flow
- Occasional advisor to Jaxon Irongear on magical or ethical concerns

The people of [[Beruda]] know her as Mistress Nell, a respected consultant with uncanny weather prediction skills. Only a select few know her draconic nature.

---

## Known Abilities

> Note: These abilities are rarely displayed openly.

- *Atmospheric Manipulation* – Can create storms, dissipate fog, generate cloud platforms, or redirect wind currents
- *Mana Pressure Calibration* – Adjusts the Weave's atmospheric density to stabilize floating structures
- *Draconic Breath (Cold)* – Used only in extreme emergencies
- *Shapeshifting* – Appears as a pale, silver-haired human mage of indeterminate age
- *Arcane Insight* – Deep understanding of magical geometry, mana tides, and resonance

---

## Relationships

- *[[Jaxon Irongear]]* – Longtime collaborator, unlikely friend. She calls him "that bastard Irongear" when he overreaches. Still, she values his mind and conviction.
- *[[Skyfleet]]* – Trusted consultant, though she avoids direct leadership roles.
- *The People of Beruda* – Sees them as fragile but worthy of protection when properly led.
