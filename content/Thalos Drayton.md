Thalos Drayton is a reclusive and eccentric arcanist known for his unorthodox experiments and skyborne lifestyle. A long-time associate of [[Jaxon Irongear]], Thalos lives in the [[Floating Cloud Castle]], a structure suspended in the sky through a mix of weather magic and arcane anchoring, built by the silver dragon [[Nell]]. Though human in origin, Thalos is often mistaken for a half-mad celestial scholar due to his unkempt appearance and sweeping philosophical declarations.

Background

Thalos studied at the [[Eidric Collegium]] in [[Mumperath]], where he distinguished himself early with treatises on spell inversion and multi-planar harmonic fields. After a scandal involving the accidental transmutation of the Dean’s tongue into a sentient snail, Thalos left the Collegium and pursued independent research.

Role in Iedas

Thalos serves as one of the quiet contributors to [[Skyfleet]], providing experimental propulsion designs and spell-array matrices for long-distance flight. His inventions are often borderline unusable by sane minds, but they are undeniably brilliant. He communicates with [[Jaxon Irongear]] through enchanted silver geese, each carrying blueprints or half-finished pastries with diagrams scribbled on them.

He pays local lords handsomely for permission to conduct field experiments, many of which result in mild atmospheric anomalies or sudden changes in goat behavior. Most lords consider his presence a blessing—for their coffers, if not their livestock.

Relationship with Nell

Thalos and [[Nell]], the enigmatic silver dragon and weather mage, share a bond of curiosity and mischief. It was Nell who offered Thalos the Floating Cloud Castle, ostensibly to keep him from “staining the earth below with too many mistakes.” Their friendship is old and strange, defined by arcane debates shouted over thunderstorms and late-night gambling using lightning bolts as currency.
