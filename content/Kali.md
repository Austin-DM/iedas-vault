[[Kali]]

Also Known As: The Red Mist
Former Title: Gebura, Silver Sword of Gwyn
Origin: [[Plane of Radiance]]
Affiliation: Former member of the [[Silver Swords of Gwyn]], currently unaffiliated
Status: Active; location unknown

⸻

Summary

Kali is a former Silver Sword of Gwyn, originally born on the [[Plane of Radiance]] and recognized early for her exceptional magical aptitude. Her birth name was Kali, but she was given the title Gebura upon joining the order. She was born a [[Blessed children|Blessed Child]], marked by the Blessing of the Hundredfold Experience, a rare and formidable gift allowing her to simulate a hundred variations of a single action before committing to one in real time.

Over time, Kali became increasingly conflicted between her divine mission and her developing sense of personal identity. Her desire for autonomy led her to seek out a way to separate herself from the burden of obedience instilled by the Silver Sword order. During her time on the material plane, she encountered [[Jaxon Irongear]], who was able to assist in a metaphysical division of her identity. The result was the creation of a new individual, [[Gebura]], composed of the duty-bound remnants of her former self. Kali retained the original blessing, body, and memories, while Gebura received a diminished version of the blessing and now works within [[Skyfleet]].

Kali is no longer listed in Radiant records, and her sword has since become unbound.

⸻

Blessing of the Hundredfold Experience

Kali’s blessing allows her to experience up to 100 versions of an action in a phantom or simulated state before executing it in reality. These rehearsals occur in a moment but are perceived in sequence by her alone. She may choose to repeat the same action (stacking potency) or test variations (optimization). The result is either an amplified effect or a maximally efficient execution.

If fewer than 50 of the simulated actions succeed, the real action fails. Success rates affect power or impact. She remembers the sensory and emotional weight of each failed trial, including pain, death, and regret.

While [[Gebura]] inherited a fragment of this ability, the full blessing remains with Kali.

⸻

Personality

Kali is reserved and independent, preferring action to discussion. She is not cruel but operates with detachment, shaped by countless simulated experiences that others cannot see. Her speech is direct and minimal. She avoids attachment and organizational allegiance, though she occasionally allies with individuals who share her goals.

⸻

Appearance

Kali wears practical, unadorned armor. She avoids any sigils or markings associated with her past. The moniker “Red Mist” comes from the aftermath of her fully empowered actions, which often leave visible vapor trails or explosive results. She carries no official weapon of the order, as her original blade became unbound following her separation from Gebura.

⸻

Relationship to the Silver Sword Order

Kali is no longer recognized by the [[Silver Swords of Gwyn]], and her name has been formally removed from their archives. As her identity was split, neither half fulfills the requirements of a proper Silver Sword. Her former blade is inert and has not selected a new bearer.

⸻

Relationship to Jaxon Irongear

Kali met [[Jaxon Irongear]] during a time of crisis, when her internal conflict with duty reached a breaking point. While Jaxon was not uniquely capable of splitting a soul, he had the tools, magical knowledge, and willingness to assist. As part of their agreement, Kali retained autonomy and her blessing, while Irongear took custody of [[Gebura]], who now works as a Skyfleet fixer. The two maintain mutual respect, though they are not closely associated.
