
Mordeus

Mordeus is the name of both a volcanic mountain at the center of the continent of [[Iedas]] and the mythic figure or primordial force associated with the mountain. The term refers interchangeably to the physical landmark and the metaphysical entity believed to shape the fate and power structures of [[Iedas]].

⸻

The Mountain
	•	Location: Precisely at the geographical heart of Iedas.
	•	Structure: Mordeus is a massive stratovolcano, visible for hundreds of miles in all directions. Its upper regions are shrouded in thick ash and magical turbulence.
	•	Activity: The volcano is perpetually active, though full-scale eruptions are rare. It vents heat, smoke, and glowing magma streams constantly, creating unpredictable tectonic and magical activity in surrounding regions.
	•	Geological Role: It serves as the continent’s magical and geological focal point, akin to a leyline convergence hub. Many believe all magic on Iedas flows from or is calibrated to Mordeus in some way.

⸻

Magma and Crown-Forging
	•	The magma of Mordeus is not ordinary lava. It contains dense concentrations of [[magicules]] and is used in the [[Crowning Ritual]], the process by which every individual on Iedas is allowed to forge a single personal crown in their lifetime.
	•	Crown-forging is only performed by trained Magmatic Mages, who interpret the silica content, gold-to-iron ratio, and magical resonance of the magma during the ritual to determine what kind of crown is to be formed.
	•	The ratio of gold to iron in a crown is significant:
	•	More gold = Magical potency, spiritual weight, metaphysical influence.
	•	More iron = Physical authority, leadership influence, worldly power.
	•	These crowns are unique to the individual but are also used to validate and verify rulership or influence, even if they are not directly political.

⸻

Religious and Mythic Status
	•	Mordeus is not worshiped as a god, but rather revered as a primordial power, something closer to a titan or fundamental world-force.
	•	It is said that the will of Mordeus is revealed through the composition of its magma, allowing for divination and long-term prophecy when interpreted by skilled seers.
	•	There is no known clergy or organized religion dedicated to Mordeus, but many nobles, magmatic orders, and ascetics dedicate their lives to understanding its power.
	•	In many cultural traditions, Mordeus is seen as the source of the continent’s legitimacy and destiny. Some believe that the first king of Iedas will be the one to wear the [[Crown of Mordeus]]—a mythical, never-forged crown that emerges directly from the volcano under conditions never observed.

⸻

Political and Magical Influence
	•	[[Nobles]] who undergo the [[Ritual of Ascendancy]]—a formal rite that grants magical or political boons—typically do so under the authority of Mordeus, even if not performed on the mountain itself. The ritual’s legitimacy is believed to derive from Mordeus.
	•	[[magicules]] surrounding Mordeus are unstable and dense, making spellcasting near the mountain unpredictable but also incredibly potent for those capable of managing it.
