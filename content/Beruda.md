


Beruda is a fortified city located at the only viable land passage into the southeastern region of the continent of [[Iedas]]. It serves as both a military checkpoint and a trade conduit, given the otherwise uninhabitable and barren nature of the lands to the south and east.

Geographic and Strategic Location
	•	Positioned at the mouth of a natural corridor leading inland from the coastal cliffs, Beruda controls access between the interior of [[Iedas]] and the largely impassable southeastern wastelands.
	•	Its location makes it a natural chokepoint and one of the last defensible bastions before entering the southeastern badlands.
	•	A large river runs nearby, supplying the city with water and powering sections of its industry.
	•	Farmland to the north and west supports a modest agricultural output, though much of the city’s food is imported due to the rocky soil.

Civic Structure
	•	Beruda is governed by a local lordship, currently influenced heavily by [[Jaxon Irongear]], who operates semi-independently within the city through his control of the [[Grand Workshop]] and the Sky Docks.
	•	Civic life is shaped around labor, innovation, and defense. Residents are accustomed to military presence and experimental technology.
	•	The population includes tradespeople, artificers, miners, farmers, mercenaries, and transient merchants.

Key Sites
	•	The [[Grand Workshop]] – A massive industrial and arcane research facility overseen by [[Jaxon Irongear]]. The Workshop employs hundreds of locals and is a dominant force in Beruda’s economy and infrastructure.
	•	The Sky Docks – A secretive and heavily warded construction yard on the northern edge of the city. Skyship are built and stored here under the pretense of “magical study.” Access is strictly limited, and most citizens believe it to be dangerous experimental territory.
	•	Inner Ring – A semi-fortified administrative and residential district where minor nobles, artisans, and influential citizens live.
	•	Outer Ring – A looser sprawl of worker housing, supply depots, livestock pens, and taverns catering to travelers and laborers.
	•	Riverfront Mills – Water-powered workshops and grain-processing facilities line the nearby river. The mills are essential to the city’s food and manufacturing sectors.

Military Presence
	•	The local garrison is well-equipped and trained, with a strong focus on defensive warfare and anti-intrusion magic due to Beruda’s strategic importance.
	•	Jaxon’s presence bolsters the military with magically reinforced constructs, arcane surveillance systems, and prototypes deployed for field testing.

Cultural Notes
	•	The city maintains a pragmatic and utilitarian culture. Displays of wealth are seen as suspicious or impractical.
	•	Residents tend to be skeptical of outsiders and favor individuals who contribute materially to the city’s functioning.
	•	Experimental failures are accepted as part of life, given the strong presence of magical research and invention.

Relationship with the Rest of Iedas
	•	Beruda serves as a buffer zone between civilization and the hazards of the southeast.
	•	It has direct communication with several inland noble families, but otherwise maintains semi-autonomy under the guise of regional stability.
	•	The city is commonly viewed as a “testing ground” by nobles and inventors alike, especially those seeking to pilot innovations far from public scrutiny.
