

The Expedition refers to the formal voyage from the continent of [[Thelum]] to [[Iedas]], initiated after the legendary dragon [[Imveanherno]] was confronted by a group of adventurers during a campaign later known as Resolution. These adventurers, predecessors to the current player characters, failed to defeat Imveanherno. However, during or after the battle, the dragon became aware of another continent to the west—its exact method of discovery remains uncertain. Abandoning his lair, Imveanherno left behind a carved map showing the known world with an added western landmass. This detail led to the launch of the first deliberate attempt to reach the unexplored continent.

The voyage was organized and funded by the Lathandrian Church, which holds substantial political and religious power across Thelum. Lance Hordecroft, a paladin of Lathander known for his pomp, wealth, and righteousness, was chosen to lead the expedition. His leadership, while competent, was marked by self-importance and excessive display. The stated goals of the expedition were both religious and geopolitical: to explore the new land, to bring civilization and divine order under the banner of Lathander, and to secure any resources or knowledge found there for Thelumite interests.

The expedition included various specialists, clergy, laborers, mercenaries, and adventurers. Among them were the individuals who now comprise the party known as [[Team Axel]]. Each had their own reason for joining. Kia, for instance, boarded to escape involvement in a religious war fomented by her parents. Others were motivated by politics, survival, curiosity, or the promise of a new life on foreign soil.

Roughly halfway across the ocean, the fleet encountered a massive tower of unnatural material—dark, polished stone that extended hundreds of feet into the sky. The tower was uncharted and made no physical sense in its structure. A small team, including the player characters, entered to investigate. Inside, they discovered a range of metaphysical chambers, conceptual rooms with unknowable architecture, and ultimately a vast ballroom housing a magical trial. Completion of the trial resulted in each participant receiving a mysterious curse linked to one of the four cardinal directions. Immediately afterward, the group was transported to the continent of Iedas, deposited without warning into the wilderness near [[Easthaven]], disoriented and alone.

The rest of the expedition—those who remained aboard the fleet—was left without knowledge of the disappearance. As of the current campaign timeline, the fleet is presumed to still be en route, likely aiming for the small island of [[Renan]] in the [[Boreal Waters]], which offers the most accessible anchorage on Iedas’s southeastern coast. The island is one of the few documented locations with any semblance of civilization in that region.

No formal contact has yet been re-established between the stranded adventurers and the larger expedition. Thelum remains entirely unknown to the people of Iedas. The arrival of the full expedition, when it occurs, is expected to alter the continent’s geopolitical landscape in unpredictable ways.


Addendum: The Fleet and Its Purpose

The Thelumite expedition was composed of seven massive galleons, each outfitted with arcane instrumentation, supplies, and personnel for long-term colonization efforts. These ships traveled in a dispersed line formation across the western sea, maximizing visual range and communication coverage across the horizon. This formation allowed the fleet to better scout for landfall while avoiding concentrated risk from natural or magical threats.

Each vessel had its own designated function, from supply and transportation to magical research and infrastructure planning. One or two of the ships have since gone silent—no distress signals, no magical pings—leaving their fate uncertain. Whether they were lost to sea, sabotage, or planar interference remains unknown.

The second galleon carried the party, along with Lance Hordecroft, one of the leading religious figures of the expedition and a prominent agent of the Lathandrian Church. His presence aboard lent both prestige and pressure to that vessel’s responsibilities, which included reconnaissance, establishing initial diplomatic contact, and securing a location suitable for an anchor colony.

There was never a predetermined landing site. The expedition’s intent was to survey the coastline and land at the first promising location. Magical communication between ships is ongoing, though compartmentalized for security. Several ships are carrying teleportation engineers whose purpose is to establish stable planar corridors between Thelum and Iedas—making this not just a voyage, but the first thread in stitching two continents together.


The Seven Galleons of the Thelumite Expedition

The fleet consisted of seven galleons, each outfitted for a specific purpose in the crossing and colonization of [[Iedas]]. Though all vessels shared common systems of arcane propulsion, planar shielding, and scry-blocked communication, each was staffed and equipped differently according to its designated mission.

1. The Dawnsworn – The lead vessel, carrying high-ranking Lathandrian clergy, magical surveyors, and several representatives from Thelumite noble houses. Its mission is to serve as the moral and diplomatic centerpiece of any initial contact with indigenous powers. The Dawnsworn also contains the largest archive and religious sanctum aboard any of the ships.

2. The Radiant March – This is the vessel the party arrived on. Designed as a forward operations platform, it carries mixed personnel: soldiers, researchers, and “volunteers” from various Thelumite factions. It was intended to conduct initial landfall, set up a safe zone, and assess both magical and ecological threats. [[Lance Hordecroft]] and the player characters were aboard this ship when they encountered the teleportation tower.

3. The Ember Vault – This ship houses the expedition’s primary arcane research division. Its lower decks include sealed laboratories, elemental testing chambers, and a stabilized keylines node harvested from Thelum. The Vault’s researchers are tasked with studying [[magicules]] in the Iedan environment and ensuring stable teleportation anchors for return travel.

4. The Gilded Barrow – A logistics vessel carrying seeds, livestock embryos, construction materials, and trade goods. Its purpose is the establishment of long-term settlements and trade outposts. The Gilded Barrow also includes diplomatic gear—coinage presses, translation circles, and supply stores for forming treaties or economic footholds.

5. The Resolute Chain – A military vessel with a fully outfitted garrison, siege-grade constructs, and a cargo of alchemical weapons and defense wards. The Chain’s crew are veterans of the border wars in Thelum and were meant to defend any established colony from unknown threats or hostile actors.

6. The Lanternwake – Designed as a planar anchor ship, the Lanternwake contains the expedition’s most advanced magical equipment, including dimensional stabilizers, anchor pylons, and teams of planar cartographers. Its primary role is to establish a network of teleportation gates between Iedas and Thelum once landfall is secure. (MISSING)

7. The Veilcutter – A stealth ship assigned to reconnaissance and silent surveillance. It sails slightly apart from the main formation and is warded against both magical and mundane detection. The Veilcutter was meant to identify potential threats before the rest of the fleet arrives. It has not reported in since the fleet neared Iedas, and its current status is unknown. (MISSING)

Each ship’s fate is currently unclear. While most remain in magical contact, one or two are believed to be lost or unreachable. The party’s interaction with the Radiant March marks the first direct fallout of the tower’s interference—and the beginning of the fleet’s fragmented entry into Iedas.
