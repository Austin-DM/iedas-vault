Floating Cloud Castle

Perched eternally above the shifting winds north of [[Beruda]], the Floating Cloud Castle is a marvel of magical engineering and weather manipulation. Constructed and maintained by the silver dragon [[Nell]], the castle drifts on controlled air currents and pressure envelopes, shielded from scrying and elemental turbulence by a network of sigils embedded into its foundation.

The castle serves as a private haven for arcane experiments and long-term research by powerful allies of [[Skyfleet]], most notably the enigmatic mage [[Thalos Drayton]], who conducts his projects here with the permission of local lords. [[Jaxon Irongear]] played a crucial role in granting Thalos access to the site, cementing a decades-long bond between himself and Nell.

While the true layout is unknown to most, the castle reportedly contains:
	•	A central observatory designed for astral alignment and weather forecasting.
	•	A network of skywalks and anchor runes that allow the structure to remain in a stable aerial position.
	•	A teleportation circle tuned to both [[Beruda]] and the inner sanctum of the [[Grand Workshop]].
	•	Several laboratories dedicated to studying mana weather, aerial leyline flux, and elemental interaction.

Nell herself rarely hosts guests, preferring solitude or conversations with those she considers intellectually curious or personally trusted. Some mages consider receiving an invitation to the Floating Cloud Castle to be a career-defining event.

Though not officially part of the [[Skyfleet]] command structure, the castle functions as an auxiliary base of operations and a sanctuary for those in need of untraceable refuge or high-altitude perspective—both literal and figurative.
