

Renan is a small, frostbitten island located in the southeastern archipelago off the coast of [[Iedas]], surrounded by the frigid expanse known as the [[Boreal Waters]]. It is the easternmost known settlement of the continent, and the closest inhabited point to the phenomenon now known as the [[Scourge of Winter]].

Though much of the island has been overtaken by creeping ice over the past decade, a small population continues to survive along the elevated northern cliffs, where the frost’s reach is slowest. The people of Renan are fiercely self-reliant and wary of outsiders. Cut off from most trade routes and ignored by larger powers, their culture has evolved in partial isolation, blending old traditions with strange adaptations to the encroaching cold.

Little is known about Renan’s origins. The settlement appears in only a handful of older maps, often marked as a mere supply station or a whaling outpost. Its significance has grown only recently as the Scourge of Winter continues to spread. Now, it serves as a tenuous foothold for researchers, scouts, and opportunists hoping to understand—or exploit—the forces behind the unnatural frost.

The island’s landscape is split between sheer, icy ridges and dark, bitter forests that seem to resist both sun and sorcery. The southern end of the island, once home to a second village and a series of coastal docks, is believed to be entirely frozen over. Travel to that region is considered suicidal.

Renan has no standing government. Authority is loosely held by a council of elders and surviving landholders, most of whom command influence through resources and the ability to coordinate supply shipments. Magical communications with the mainland are sporadic due to interference from the Scourge, and only ships bearing magical protection can safely reach the harbor during colder months.

As of late, the island has attracted attention from [[Skyfleet]], whose agents have proposed setting up a staging ground or observational post on the island. This initiative, if completed, may mark the beginning of Renan’s reentry into continental affairs—or its final descent into silence.
