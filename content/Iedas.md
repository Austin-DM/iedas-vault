
Iedas is a vast and largely untamed continent in the world of [[Threntin]]. Much of its terrain remains uncharted, marked by ancient magic, elemental instability, and extreme geographic variation. Unlike Thelum, Iedas has no central kingdom or empire. Its social structure is decentralized, with authority divided among [[Nobles]] who govern independently and derive their power through magical and spiritual means.

Political Structure

Power in Iedas is not inherited in the traditional feudal sense but earned through the [[Ritual of Ascendancy]], a sacred communion with [[Mordeus]]—the elemental force that resides within the massive volcano at the heart of the continent. Through this ritual, Nobles are granted blessings that enhance their physical, magical, or spiritual influence. These gifts vary in potency based on status, heritage, and the interpretation of Mordeus’s will. While some nobles gain combative or elemental powers, others receive more esoteric or symbolic abilities.

Mordeus and the Crown

The volcano [[Mordeus]] dominates central Iedas, both geographically and mythologically. The [[Crown of Mordeus]], a radiant formation of molten iron, gold, and ash suspended above the crater, serves as both a spiritual symbol and a prophetic mystery. No one has claimed the Crown, but its presence inspires pilgrimage, research, and political aspiration across the land.

One of Iedas’s most sacred traditions is the forging of personal [[crowns]] in the magma of Mordeus. These crowns are made once in a lifetime and represent the bearer’s destiny. The ratio of gold to iron in a crown symbolizes one’s alignment toward magical influence versus worldly authority. The shape, gemstone type, and spire count of a crown are interpreted by trained magmatic mages to understand one’s fate and societal role.

Geography

The eastern coast of Iedas is relatively fertile and populated. Settlements like [[Beruda]] and [[Easthaven]] provide access to fishing, trade, and early infrastructure. As one moves inland, civilization gives way to wild landscapes. The center is dominated by volcanic and mountainous terrain, while the far north is a realm of stone towers and shifting earth, shaped by the domain of an earth-aligned elemental lord.

The far west transitions into vast flatlands, home to a now-dormant martial culture that once trained cavalry warriors using flaming blades. Meanwhile, the eastern seas are freezing over due to a phenomenon known as the [[Scourge of Winter]], caused by an ice elemental whose domain is slowly expanding. The western ocean is influenced by a water elemental whose territory is growing increasingly volatile. A storm elemental may also exist—its presence possibly in conflict with the water elemental, though this remains unconfirmed.

Magic

Magic in Iedas is derived from the manipulation of [[magicules]]—ambient arcane particles present in all things. These particles can be shaped into spells through willpower, intention, geometry, or ritual, depending on the caster’s tradition. While all spellcasters follow the same fundamental magical processes (whether they know it or not), they experience and interpret those processes differently. This is detailed in the article on [[spellcasting]].

The manipulation of the [[Weave]], the underlying field that magicules adhere to, is central to all casting. Theories about the Weave vary, from the metaphysical to the mathematical, but all agree it governs the structure and behavior of magic itself.

Blessed and Cursed Children

Children occasionally emerge who are altered by Iedas itself. These are known as [[Blessed children]] and [[Cursed children]]. Blessed children are considered to have been “added to”—imbued with gifts beyond the norm. Their abilities may be subtle or overwhelming, and often reflect the magical ecology or history of the region in which they were born.

Cursed children are seen as having had something stolen from them—whether emotionally, physically, or spiritually. In exchange, the world often compensates them with a unique power or perception, though not always a welcome one.

In both cases, such children are treated with awe, fear, or reverence, depending on the culture and region.

Current Events

The expedition from [[Thelum]] recently made landfall, spurred by the discovery of Iedas after a failed battle against the ancient dragon [[Imveanherno]]. The players were part of this expedition, which encountered a mysterious tower in the ocean that scattered them across the land after a magical event tied to the [[cardinal curses]].

Now settled in [[Beruda]], the party has been recruited by [[Jaxon Irongear]] into a secretive project called [[Skyfleet]], a covert aerial initiative aimed at defending Iedas from rising threats—including the elemental lords and potential political collapse.

Culture

Iedas places high value on individuality and destiny, embodied through crowns, the Ascendancy, and the myths surrounding Mordeus. Nobles are expected to rule not by inheritance alone, but through demonstrable connection to the land’s magical and spiritual forces. Power here is personal, divine, and deeply symbolic.

The common people often lack access to magical education but still understand the significance of a noble’s crown, the meaning of an elemental storm, or the implications of a [[Blessed children|Blessing]] or [[Cursed children|curse]]. Their respect for these elements—while not always rooted in formal knowledge—is sincere and enduring.
